# QA: look before you pay for the next step

Numbers catch what a script can measure. Eyes catch the rest: a wrong flag, a caption over a
face, three beats that look the same. Run both, after every generation and again on the master.
The scripts are in `scripts/`; the checklists below say what each one cannot see.

## The verify loop

After EVERY generation, before spending on the next, open the artifact: view images directly,
build a contact sheet for any video (`scripts/contact_sheet.py`), read the numbers each script
prints. Then give a verdict:

- pass: proceed;
- retry: a content defect (wrong subject, wrong symbol, garbled text, morphing, bad take).
  Diagnose why, change the prompt or the script, regenerate once. Never resubmit identical input;
- rework free: anything about timing, captions, layout, mix or encode goes back to assembly,
  never to a paid call.

A beat that fails twice moves down a layer (`00-layer-doctrine.md`). Record each verdict in your
notes and any accepted defect in `beatmap.json`.

## Per-asset checks

**Beat map and animatic (free).** `beatmap_lint.py` exits 0; warnings each have a decision.
Watch `animatic.mp4`: is the pace right, does any layer or device repeat for too long, does the
hook read in 3 s, does the payoff close the hook's loop?

**Voice.** Measured duration within about 25% of target, otherwise rewrite words and re-synthesise
(cents). Leading silence at most 0.7 s (the QC hook gate is 0.9 s). Listen to the hook and the
reveal for performance. Numbers and names pronounced correctly. A native listen for any non-English
language.

**Still.** No text, letters or numbers anywhere. No wrong-country tells: flag or emblem, script,
driving side, vehicles, plugs, uniforms, banknotes, architecture, vegetation, stereotyped dress.
Subject matches what the narration literally says. Lower third calm for captions. Same grade and
grain as the other frames. Counts right (people, steps). Left and right not swapped. No hands
unless required.

**Clip.** Open the contact sheet: on-topic, no wrong symbols, no morphing or melting edges, no
extra text, no duplicated objects, one camera move, start and end frames similar if it loops, no
strobing. Run `qc_master.py` on it for dead frames and flicker.

**Host.** Duration close to the voice; the same face in every frame; framing steady; the lower third
clear; mouth motion alive. Lip sync cannot be judged from stills: caption coverage at QC and a
listen are the proxies. A broken take is a content defect; a timing problem waits for assembly.

**Code beat.** Frame sampled at the landing and at the hold: nothing off-frame or clipped, text at
the size ladder, contrast met, numbers equal the claim, chart bars proportional, equation fits the
width. The hero is the biggest changed region (`05-motion-and-edit-grammar.md`). HyperFrames'
`check` covers overflow and contrast; Manim needs the geometry audit and a look at z-order.

**Words (alignment).** Word count within about 15% of the script; read the first and last five words
against the script. A gross mismatch means the wrong audio file or an improvising host.

## Master checks

`qc_master.py master.mp4 --beatmap beatmap.json --platform tiktok` reports spec, duration,
audio/video delta, hook audio, loudness, dead frames, flicker, pacing, and loop (`--loop`). Pacing fails only a long shot in which
nothing moves; a talking host, an animated diagram or a moving overlay is movement, and seamless jump cuts count as none. Never
edit the picture to satisfy a check (see "The bar" below).
Then these checks need eyes or extra measurement. Q1, Q4 and Q5 are measured by `scripts/layout_check.py --page build/index.html`
(text size, the safe box and text collisions from the page's DOM at every beat); `contact_sheet.py` leads with the hook frame at 0.4 s
for Q11 and tints the danger bands lightly:

| # | Check | Method | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | text size | measure the rendered font size of every text element | chart labels at least 40 px; captions at least 48 px; source line at least 36 px; hero at least 140 px |
| Q2 | text contrast | sample the background under each text box across the whole hold; worst case against the text colour | at least 4.5:1 (3:1 only above about 66 px regular or 52 px bold); else scrim 0.55 or more, stroke or box |
| Q3 | text duration | hold versus `0.29 s x words + entrance` | at least the formula (CJK 0.25 s per character) |
| Q4 | safe zones and overflow | contact sheet overlay; boxes inside the common safe box | no hero, caption, face or key data in a red band |
| Q5 | collisions | pairwise text and chart box intersection at sampled times | none except designed (a label on its own bar) |
| Q6 | face occlusion | any face detector or a look | no graphic over the eye and mouth region |
| Q7 | sync | calibration cue: flash plus 1 kHz click at a known time, measure both | within 1 frame; audio never more than 1 frame early |
| Q8 | dead and partial frames | `qc_master` plus contact sheets around cuts | no black, freeze beyond the plan, half-transitions, or a layer popping 1 frame early |
| Q9 | banding | crop dark gradients from a decoded frame | longest flat run under about 100 of 960 rows; use static grain |
| Q10 | palette | accent hex across sampled frames | one accent hue per episode |
| Q11 | hook | contact sheet at 0.4 s with sound off | frame 0 already carries the hook visual; sound within 0.3 s |
| Q12 | loop | first and last frame side by side | the last frame echoes the first |
| Q13 | audio | `ebur128=peak=true` | -14 plus or minus 0.5 LUFS; true peak at most -1 dBTP; no digital silence over 0.3 s |
| Q14 | chart honesty | data table versus bar heights | zero baseline; heights proportional within 2%; unit and source visible |
| Q15 | script rendering | an Arabic, Urdu or Devanagari sample frame | joins intact, nothing clipped, no detached marks |
| Q16 | claims | re-read `claims[]` against the final cut | every number on screen or in voice traces to a claim; as-of dates present; scripts drift during editing |
| Q17 | disclosure | `meta.disclose` and platform flags | decided, recorded, and matches `08-governance.md` |

## The bar: ready to post

Before delivery, answer each question with evidence (a frame, a number), and fix a "no" instead of noting it:

1. Does the frame carry a human presence (a host, a real or human-grade voice) where the format allows? Is an AI-generated person disclosed?
2. Is the voice above the draft rung of the ladder (`06-sound.md`)? Did `voice_check.py` pass for it?
3. Is every text element at the Q1 sizes, inside the safe box, over enough contrast, held long enough to read?
4. Does every transition carry meaning? No flash, dip or decorative wipe.
5. Is there one hero motion per beat, and does the host never get covered at the eyes and mouth (Q6)?
6. Do captions follow the host's own words (timings from the master audio)?
7. Does the hook land with sound off and inside 3 seconds?
8. Is `provenance.json` complete (`provenance.py check`) and the AI-use statement in the delivery note?
9. Would you stop scrolling for it? Look at the contact sheet and three full-size frames as a viewer on a phone would.

## Sampling and commands

| Sheet | Frames | Why |
|---|---|---|
| overview | every 1 s, plus every 0.5 s in the first 3 s; 270 px wide, timestamps burned in | pacing, palette, dead stretches, hook |
| cut neighbourhoods | each planned cut and hero landing: -2, -1, 0, +1, +2, +6, +12 frames | off-by-one, flash frames, partial transitions |
| safe zone | one frame per beat at its hold, with the platform overlay | text and face placement against UI |
| loop | first and last frame side by side | continuity |
| legibility crops | each text region at 100% | real contrast and edge quality |

```bash
python3 scripts/contact_sheet.py master.mp4 --beatmap beatmap.json --spans spans.json --platform tiktok
python3 scripts/contact_sheet.py clip.mp4 --frames 6 --cols 3 --no-zones                 # one asset, raw
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "blackdetect=d=0.1:pix_th=0.10" -an -f null -                       # black frames
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -vf "freezedetect=n=-60dB:d=1" -an -f null -                            # holds (intentional ones trigger it too)
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -af "silencedetect=n=-50dB:d=0.5" -vn -f null -                         # digital silence
ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -af "ebur128=peak=true" -vn -f null -                                   # loudness and true peak
ffprobe -v error -show_entries stream=codec_type,codec_name,start_time,duration,r_frame_rate,nb_frames -of compact in.mp4
```

Sparse vector scenes need a much lower freeze threshold: on a scene with one moving dot,
`freezedetect=n=0.0005` falsely reported a freeze from t=0 while `n=0.000005` found the real 5 s
hold. The `scene` filter scores the change in change, so periodic strobing registers once: use
luma difference (`signalstats` YDIF), which `qc_master.py` does.

## Repair: free versus paid

Assembly-class failures are free: duration trimming by script words (then re-synth the voice, which is
cents), audio/video delta, dead frames from a segment gap, loudness, caption restyle, safe-zone
moves, overlay timing. Content-class failures cost money: a wrong visual, a bad take, a strobing clip.
Fix the plan first, regenerate the minimum asset (one beat), and ledger it.

| QC failure | Repair |
|---|---|
| duration | words are the knob: rewrite, re-synth, only then regenerate anything expensive; never stretch audio |
| audio/video delta | re-mux with `-shortest` or pin `-t`; persistent means a stream was clipped or padded upstream |
| hook audio | trim leading silence at the source or tighten beat 0; an avatar engine sometimes adds preamble |
| loudness | re-run the mix with the two-pass recipe; check the music is not clipping the voice |
| dead frames | black is a segment gap: compare clip durations with beat spans; freeze is a stuck loop or a held last frame |
| flicker | a strobing generated clip is a content defect: regenerate that beat once with a changed prompt, or downgrade it |
| spec | re-encode: 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz, `+faststart`; re-mux if start times differ |
| pacing | add a graphic or motion change, or cut |
| loop | make the last beat's final frame echo the first |

## Series checks

For an episode of a series, add these to the per-asset and master checks. The script covers what it can measure; the eye covers the rest.

| Check | By | What it cannot see |
|---|---|---|
| language, cast and format match the series; the look and the cast are unchanged since the lock | `series.py check` | whether the lock itself was a good decision |
| this episode's declared changes are real; hook and treatment are not repeats of the window | `series.py check`, against the limits the user set | a repeat the numbers miss: same joke, new words |
| gags counted, threads and cliffhangers paid off | `series.py check` | whether a callback lands |
| every character frame matches the canonical sheet, within the pass mark and not above the copy-paste ceiling | `series.py calibrate` for the marks, an encoder you may legally use for the scores, then your eyes on a contact sheet of the cast | an encoder blind to this art style (the calibration says so when it is) |
| voices are the stored ones | the speaker lines in `SCRIPT.md`, a listen | a voice that drifted inside the engine |
| punchlines were chosen or rewritten by the human | the Gate 1 record | whether it is funny: watch the animatic |
| the series budget holds | `series.py rollup` | next episode's price drift |

## Final review

Producers have confirmation bias. If sub-agents are available, one fresh-context reviewer gets only
the master, the contact sheet, `claims[]` and the QC report, and returns numbered findings with
timestamps and a concrete fix each (`08-governance.md`). Without that, take a break from the plan and
review the frames against Q1 to Q17 before you report. Deliver the master, the duration, the ledger
total, the sources, a captions file (SRT or VTT), the disclosure note, and one line on anything accepted
or degraded.
